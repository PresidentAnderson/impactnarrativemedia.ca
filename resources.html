Kids Help Phone

24/7 counselling for young people. Call, text, or chat online. Free, confidential, judgment-free.

1-800-668-6868 · Text CONNECT to 686868 · All Canada

Food Banks Canada

Find your nearest food bank. Over 5,000 locations across Canada. No judgment, no paperwork at most locations.

foodbankscanada.ca · All provinces

Shelter Safe

Find emergency shelters for women and children fleeing violence. Confidential directory of safe spaces across Canada.

sheltersafe.ca · All provinces

Mental Health

BounceBack

Free CBT-based program for adults and youth experiencing low mood, anxiety, or depression. Phone and online coaching.

bouncebackontario.ca · ON, BC, MB, NS, QC
Immigration

Immigration Refugee Board Helpline

Information about refugee claims, hearings, and your rights in the immigration system.

1-800-667-6397 · All Canada
Indigenous Services

Hope for Wellness Helpline

Immediate mental health counselling and crisis intervention for all Indigenous peoples. Available in English, French, Cree, Ojibway, and Inuktitut.

1-855-242-3310 · All Canada · 24/7
Youth

Covenant House

Emergency shelter and services for homeless youth aged 16-24. Crisis line, drop-in, and long-term housing support.

covenanthousebc.org · Vancouver, Toronto
