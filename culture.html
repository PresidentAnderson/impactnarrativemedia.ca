March Book Club Pick

Moon of the Crusted Snow

By Waubgeshig Rice · Published by ECW Press

When the power goes out and the supply chain collapses, a remote Anishinaabe community falls back on traditional knowledge to survive. A gripping, genre-bending novel about resilience, sovereignty, and what happens when the structures we depend on disappear.

Why we chose it: This book sits at the intersection of so many themes we cover — Indigenous sovereignty, climate crisis, community resilience, and the fragility of the systems we take for granted. Plus it's a page-turner.
