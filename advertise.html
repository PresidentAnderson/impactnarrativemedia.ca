Frequently Asked Questions

Do advertisers influence your editorial content?

No. Never. Our editorial team operates independently. Advertisers have zero input on what we cover or how we cover it. This is non-negotiable.

Do you use tracking or programmatic ads?

No. We don't track readers, sell data, or use programmatic ad networks. Your ad appears on our site, clearly labelled, without any surveillance attached.

Are there businesses you won't accept ads from?

Yes. We don't accept advertising from fossil fuel companies, predatory lenders, gambling operations, or organizations whose practices contradict our editorial mission. We reserve the right to decline any ad.

Can I get a discount for multiple months?

Yes. 3-month commitments get 10% off, 6-month get 15%, and annual gets 25% off. Non-profits get an additional 20% discount on all packages.

Do you provide ad creative services?

We can help! Basic ad design is included with display banner packages. For custom creative, we charge a one-time $75 design fee.
