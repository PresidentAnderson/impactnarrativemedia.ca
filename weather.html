Active Advisories

Extreme Cold Warning
Nunavut, Northwest Territories, northern Manitoba

Wind chill values near −45. Frostbite can develop within minutes on exposed skin. Environment and Climate Change Canada urges limiting outdoor exposure. Warming centres open in Iqaluit, Yellowknife, and Churchill.

Winter Storm Watch
Nova Scotia, New Brunswick, Prince Edward Island

Significant snowfall expected Friday through Saturday. 15–25cm with strong northeast winds. Power outages possible. Check on elderly neighbours and stock supplies.

Flood Watch
Fraser Valley, British Columbia

Prolonged rain and snowmelt raising water levels. Residents near the Nooksack and Sumas rivers should monitor conditions. Emergency preparedness kits recommended.

Freezing Rain Advisory
Ottawa Valley, Eastern Ontario

Freezing rain possible Thursday evening. Commuters should allow extra time. Bridges and overpasses freeze first. Drive carefully.

Weather Affects Us All Differently

Extreme weather disproportionately impacts vulnerable communities — people experiencing homelessness, elders living alone, people with disabilities, and communities without adequate infrastructure. If you or someone you know is in danger due to weather conditions, please reach out for help.

Resources: 211 Canada (dial 2-1-1) · Canadian Red Cross · Local warming centres directory · Indigenous Services Canada weather emergencies
