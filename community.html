Margaret "Peggy" Flett, 78, Still Runs the Only Grocery Store for 200km

In Churchill, Manitoba, where the highway ends and the polar bears begin, Peggy Flett has kept the shelves stocked for 42 years. Through supply chain collapses, climate change, and a pandemic that cut off the train line, she's never closed. Not once.

"People need to eat," she says, as if that explains everything. And maybe it does.

By Fatima Al-HassanMarch 24, 20268 min read

Re: "The Invisible Eviction" — This happened to my family

Your investigation hit painfully close to home. My family was renovicted from our apartment of 11 years in 2024. Three months later, the "renovated" unit was listed at triple our rent with cosmetic changes only. Reading your series, I finally felt like someone understood what happened to us. Thank you for taking the time to tell this story properly.

Sandra M., Hamilton, ONMarch 25, 2026

Rural healthcare isn't just a Saskatchewan problem

I read your investigation on rural healthcare with tears in my eyes. I'm a nurse practitioner in northern New Brunswick and the situation here is identical. We have one doctor for 4,000 people. Last month a man died of a heart attack because the nearest ER was 90 minutes away and the ambulance took 40 minutes to arrive. This is a national crisis being treated as a provincial inconvenience.

Michel T., RN(NP), Campbellton, NBMarch 24, 2026

Why we need more journalists like you covering disability

As a wheelchair user, I'm used to being invisible in Canadian media. When disability is covered at all, it's usually as inspiration or tragedy. Your reporting on the disability benefit and on aging out of care systems treated disabled people as whole humans with agency. Please keep going.

Jordan K., Vancouver, BCMarch 22, 2026

Lives Remembered

Robert "Bobby" Sinclair

1948 – 2026 · Winnipeg, MB

Cree elder, Korean War veteran, and beloved grandfather. Bobby spent 30 years as a bus driver and never passed a hitchhiker. He is survived by his wife Joan, 4 children, and 11 grandchildren who all knew the words to "Four Strong Winds."

Dr. Amina Youssef

1962 – 2026 · Halifax, NS

Family physician who served the North End community for 28 years. Arrived from Syria in 1990 and never stopped giving. Her patients say she remembered every name, every story. The clinic she built will continue in her honour.

Thomas "Old Tom" MacPherson

1935 – 2026 · Cape Breton, NS

Fisherman, fiddler, and the last man in Inverness County who could fix anything with baling wire. Tom is remembered for his laugh, which could be heard across the harbour, and his lobster chowder, the recipe for which he took with him.
