Submit Your Story

Everyone has a story. Some stories change everything. We want to help you tell yours.

Impact Narrative Media was built on the belief that the most important stories often come from the people living them. Whether you've experienced something the public needs to know about, or you're a writer with an investigation ready to publish, we want to hear from you.

Share Your Experience

You don't need to be a writer. You just need a story that matters. If you've experienced or witnessed something that the public should know about — housing injustice, healthcare failures, workplace abuse, environmental harm, systemic discrimination — we want to listen.

  • ➤ Tell us what happened in your own words
  • ➤ We'll work with you to shape the narrative
  • ➤ You control how much you're identified
  • ➤ Trauma-informed process throughout
  • ➤ You review the story before publication

Submit as a Writer

Are you a journalist, researcher, or writer with a story ready to publish? We're actively seeking investigative pieces, first-person narratives, opinion columns, and community reporting from across Canada.

  • ➤ We pay for published work
  • ➤ Investigations: $500–$2,500
  • ➤ First-person narratives: $200–$800
  • ➤ Opinion/analysis: $150–$500
  • ➤ Community reporting: $100–$400

Share Your Experience

Fill out as much or as little as you're comfortable with. Everything you share is confidential until you give explicit permission to publish.

⚠ Your safety comes first

We will never publish your name, location, or identifying details without your written consent. If sharing your story could put you at risk, let us know and we'll take extra precautions. You can also reach us via encrypted channels below.

Share as much or as little as you're comfortable with. You can write in any style — just tell us what happened and why it matters.

Documents, photos, emails, records, etc. Don't share anything here you're not comfortable with — we can discuss secure transfer later.

What happens next: An editor will review your submission within 5 business days. We'll reach out to discuss next steps, including whether we can pursue the story, what the process looks like, and how you'd like to be involved. Nothing is published without your consent.

For Writers & Journalists

We're always looking for strong investigative journalism, compelling narratives, and informed analysis from writers across Canada.

Tell us what the story is, why it matters, and what evidence or sources you have. If it's a completed piece, paste or attach the full text.

Our commitment to writers: We respond to every pitch within 10 business days. We pay on acceptance, not publication. We don't rewrite your work without discussion. And we'll always credit you prominently.
