The Invisible Eviction

How corporate landlords are systematically displacing 40,000 Canadian families through renovictions, above-guideline rent increases, and deliberate neglect. A six-month investigation across five provinces.

By Amara Okafor · March 24, 2026 · 18 min read · Part 1 of 4
⚠ Content Advisory

This investigation contains accounts of housing loss, financial distress, and impacts on children and elderly residents. Descriptions of displacement-related anxiety and depression are included.

The families featured in this series have consented to share their stories. Some names have been changed at their request. We worked with trauma-informed reporting guidelines from the Dart Center for Journalism and Trauma.
