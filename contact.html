Confidential Tips

Have information about wrongdoing, corruption, or abuse of power? We protect our sources. Here is how to reach us securely:

  • SecureDrop: Access our SecureDrop instance through the Tor Browser at our .onion address (contact us for the current URL)
  • Signal: Reach our investigations team via Signal. Contact tips@impactnarrativemedia.ca for our current Signal number.
  • Encrypted Email: Request our PGP public key at tips@impactnarrativemedia.ca
  • Mail: Send physical documents to: Investigations Desk (Confidential), Impact Narrative Media, 180 John Street, Suite 300, Toronto, ON M5T 1X5

We never reveal our sources without their explicit consent. Your identity is protected under Canadian journalistic privilege.
