Accessibility Statement

Last updated: March 26, 2026

Our Commitment

Impact Narrative Media is committed to ensuring that our website and content are accessible to all people, including those with disabilities. We believe that access to independent journalism is a fundamental right, and we strive to remove barriers that might prevent anyone from engaging with our reporting.

We are working to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, as published by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C). These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible for people with a wide range of disabilities.

Current Accessibility Features

Our website includes the following accessibility features:

Structure and Navigation

  • Semantic HTML: We use proper HTML5 semantic elements (header, nav, main, article, aside, footer) to provide meaningful document structure for assistive technologies.
  • Keyboard navigation: All interactive elements, including navigation menus, links, buttons, and forms, are fully operable using a keyboard alone.
  • Skip navigation: Skip-to-content links allow keyboard users to bypass repeated navigation elements.
  • Logical heading hierarchy: Pages use a consistent, sequential heading structure (h1 through h6) to organize content.
  • Landmark regions: ARIA landmark roles identify major sections of each page.

Visual Design

  • Colour contrast: Text and interactive elements maintain a contrast ratio of at least 4.5:1 against their backgrounds, meeting WCAG AA requirements.
  • Responsive design: The site adapts to various screen sizes, from mobile phones to large desktop monitors, ensuring readability at all viewport widths.
  • Resizable text: All text can be resized up to 200% without loss of content or functionality.
  • No colour-only indicators: Information conveyed through colour is also available through other visual cues (text labels, icons, patterns).

Assistive Technology Support

  • ARIA labels: Interactive elements and dynamic content include appropriate ARIA attributes to convey their purpose and state to screen readers.
  • Screen reader compatibility: The site is tested with common screen readers including NVDA, JAWS, and VoiceOver.
  • Alternative text: Images include descriptive alt text. Decorative images are marked appropriately so screen readers skip them.
  • Form labels: All form inputs have associated labels, and form errors are clearly communicated.

Content

  • Plain language: We strive to write in clear, plain language that is accessible to a broad audience.
  • Link text: Links use descriptive text that makes sense out of context, rather than generic "click here" language.
  • Content warnings: Articles containing potentially distressing content include content warnings as part of our trauma-aware journalism practices.

Known Limitations

While we are committed to accessibility, we acknowledge the following current limitations:

  • Third-party content: Some embedded content from third-party services (e.g., social media embeds, external forms) may not fully meet WCAG 2.1 AA standards. We are working with these providers to improve accessibility.
  • Older content: Some archived articles published before our current accessibility standards were adopted may have images without adequate alt text. We are progressively remediating this content.
  • PDF documents: Some downloadable documents may not be fully accessible. Where possible, we provide HTML alternatives. Contact us if you need an accessible version of any document.
  • Video captions: While we aim to caption all video content, some live or recently published videos may have auto-generated captions that contain errors. We review and correct captions as quickly as possible.

Requesting Accommodations

If you encounter any accessibility barrier on our site, or if you need content in an alternative format, we are here to help. We can provide:

  • Articles in plain text format
  • Large print versions of content
  • Audio descriptions of visual content
  • Accessible versions of PDF documents
  • Assistance navigating the site

Please contact us with your request, and we will do our best to respond within two (2) business days.

Feedback

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of Impact Narrative Media. If you encounter an accessibility barrier, have suggestions for improvement, or want to report an issue, please contact us:

Email: accessibility@impactnarrativemedia.ca
Mail: Accessibility Coordinator, Impact Narrative Media, 180 John Street, Suite 300, Toronto, ON M5T 1X5
Phone: Available upon request via email

When reporting an accessibility issue, please include:

  • The web address (URL) of the page where you experienced the issue
  • A description of the problem
  • The assistive technology you were using (if applicable)
  • Your preferred method of contact

Ongoing Commitment

Accessibility is not a one-time effort but an ongoing commitment. We are continuously working to improve the accessibility of our site through:

  • Regular accessibility audits using both automated tools and manual testing
  • User testing with people who have disabilities
  • Staff training on accessible content creation
  • Incorporating accessibility into our editorial and development workflows
  • Monitoring and adopting evolving accessibility standards and best practices
  • Engaging with disability communities to understand their needs

We review this accessibility statement annually and update it to reflect our progress and any changes to our practices.

Relevant Standards and Legislation

  • Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1
  • Accessible Canada Act (S.C. 2019, c. 10)
  • Accessibility for Ontarians with Disabilities Act (AODA), 2005
