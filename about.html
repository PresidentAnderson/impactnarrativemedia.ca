About Impact Narrative Media

We exist because the stories that matter most are the hardest to tell — and the most important to hear.

Our Mission

Impact Narrative Media is an independent Canadian news outlet built on a simple conviction: the people most affected by injustice should be at the centre of the story, not at its margins.

We produce deep, evidence-based investigative journalism on the issues that shape Canadian life — housing, healthcare, justice, the environment, labour, and the rights of Indigenous, Black, racialized, disabled, and marginalized communities.

Our exposés are controversial because the truth often is. We go where the story leads, take the time it requires, and report with the care and rigour that the people in our stories deserve.

Our Values

Elevate the Voiceless

The people closest to the problem understand it best. We centre their voices, their expertise, and their agency in every story we tell.

Trauma-Aware Reporting

We follow trauma-informed interviewing practices, include content advisories, and provide crisis resources. Accountability journalism doesn't require re-traumatizing people.

Deep, Not Fast

Our investigations take weeks and months, not hours. We verify exhaustively, seek multiple sources, and always offer the right of reply.

Independent & Accountable

No corporate owner. No political affiliation. We're funded by readers, advertisers, and special projects — and we publish our funding sources transparently.

Community-Rooted

From our community bulletin board to our real estate listings, we believe media should serve and connect people — not just inform them.

Controversial When Necessary

We don't seek controversy for its own sake. But we don't shy away from it when the evidence demands uncomfortable truths be told.
