Quiz #312 — March 26, 2026

Theme: Coast to Coast Knowledge

5 questions · ~3 minutes

1 How many long-term drinking water advisories are currently in effect in First Nations communities?

As of 2026, 28 long-term drinking water advisories remain in effect in First Nations communities, despite federal commitments to resolve all of them. Impact Narrative has been tracking this issue in our ongoing "Poisoned Waters" investigation.

2 Which Canadian province has the highest percentage of renters spending more than 30% of income on housing?

British Columbia leads the country, with over 45% of renters spending more than 30% of their income on housing. Nova Scotia has been rising rapidly.

3 What is the 988 number in Canada?

988 is Canada's Suicide Crisis Helpline, launched in November 2023. It provides 24/7 support in English and French for people experiencing suicidal thoughts or emotional distress.

4 Canada's Temporary Foreign Worker Program brought in approximately how many workers in 2025?

Canada's TFWP approved over 400,000 positions in 2025. Our investigation "The Migrant Worker Trap" revealed systemic issues with employer-tied permits leaving workers vulnerable to abuse.

5 What percentage of Canadians are estimated to be unable to afford home ownership as of 2026?

New data reveals 62% of Canadians are now priced out of home ownership, up from 49% in 2020. This was featured in our breaking news coverage this week.
